# Refunds policy

## Eligibility

Customers may request a full refund within **14 days** of purchase if they have not consumed more than 10% of their quota.

## Process

1. Verify purchase date in the billing dashboard.
2. Confirm usage is under the 10% threshold.
3. Approve or deny within **2 business days**.

## Non-refundable

- Enterprise annual contracts after the 14-day window.
- Add-on packs that were fully consumed.

For API billing disputes, see `docs/api-auth.md` for support contacts.
